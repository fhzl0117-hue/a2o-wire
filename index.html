SEOUL — OCTOBER 2026

A Signal From
Planet Soulite.

Two new groups, one shared universe. On October 13, 2026, A2O Entertainment — the company Lee Soo-man founded after leaving SM Entertainment — debuted nine-member girl group A2O LITE and 15-member boy group A2O SOUL side by side, with the singles "NEON" and "SUPERSTAR." Within 29 hours, "NEON" was No. 1 on Melon's Top 100, the fastest rookie-group debut song to get there since Melon revamped its chart in 2021. Both groups were introduced at a joint showcase at KINTEX in Goyang and share a sci-fi world called ACE, set on a planet named Soulite, with music and performance overseen by producer Yoo Young-jin. This page is a working build of how A2O Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking fans — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on the lineup: A2O LITE has nine members and A2O SOUL has fifteen, per the agency's debut materials as reported by Soompi and Korean outlets. Several members are very young, so this page sticks to names, music, and officially released video, and nothing more personal.

SEOUL — OCT 25

A2O LITE collect four music-show trophies for 'NEON' in 13 days

A2O LITE's debut single 'NEON' has now won on The Show (Oct. 20), Show Champion (Oct. 21), M Countdown (Oct. 22) and Inkigayo (Oct. 25), the fourth coming 13 days after the group's Oct. 12 debut showcase. It stayed at No. 1 on Melon's Top 100 for 11 straight days through Oct. 25 and topped Circle Chart's Week 42 global and digital rankings. Overseas it sat at No. 4 on Apple Music's global daily list and No. 1 on three QQ Music charts, and the group says promotions will continue across music shows.

Summarized from Sports Kyunghyang by A2O Wire staff.

SEOUL — OCT 18

A2O LITE asked for a harder 'NEON' routine, and the choreographer was stunned

Choreographer J Black says the rookies of A2O LITE did something idols seldom do: they asked him to toughen the demo routine for NEON. They also wanted his own signature style, which caught him off guard. The opening sequence is performed by Aijing and Xili, who drilled with three trainers to nail it. Reviewing the stage with his wife and fellow choreographer Mari on October 16, he credited the pair's execution for making it work.

Summarized from allkpop by A2O Wire staff.

SEOUL — OCT 15

A2O LITE takes "NEON" to its first music show on M Countdown

Barely two days after release, A2O LITE brought "NEON" to Mnet's M Countdown for its first music-show stage, scheduled for the Oct. 15 6 PM KST broadcast, as the song was still sitting at No. 1 on Melon's Top 100. Topstar News also notes that fan-made videos using the track's audio were spreading on TikTok and YouTube.

Summarized from Topstar News by A2O Wire staff.

SEOUL — OCT 14

"NEON" hits No. 1 on Melon in 29 hours, fastest rookie-group debut since 2021

A2O LITE's debut single "NEON" reached No. 1 on Melon's Top 100 at 11 PM KST on Oct. 14 — 29 hours after its 6 PM release the day before. The Korea JoongAng Daily reports it is the fastest a rookie group's debut song has topped the chart since Melon revamped it in 2021, beating the previous mark held by Allday Project's "Famous" (2025), which took about three days. It is Lee Soo-man's first new girl group for the Korean market since he left SM Entertainment in 2023.

Summarized from Korea JoongAng Daily and Soompi by A2O Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — OCT 13

A2O LITE and A2O SOUL release debut singles "NEON" and "SUPERSTAR"

Both groups released their debut singles at 6 PM KST on Oct. 13 — nine-member A2O LITE with "NEON," a bass-driven dance-pop track the company calls "Zalpha pop," and 15-member A2O SOUL with the pre-release single "SUPERSTAR." Soompi's profile-photo report names A2O LITE as Young, Oa, Ayune, Aijing, Himeki, Rada, Xili, Vora, and Merci, and A2O SOUL as Moon, Tai, Gaku, See-one, Seoha, Sono, Lucien, Zai, Ayden, Alure, Zet, Sora, Teo, June, and Hansung. The NEON music video had been pre-released the night before, at 9:30 PM KST on Oct. 12.

Summarized from Soompi by A2O Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

GOYANG — OCT 12

Global debut showcase at KINTEX introduces a shared sci-fi universe and 4D tech

A2O Entertainment held its joint debut show, "WELCOME TO SOULITE From A2O SOUL A2O LITE," at KINTEX in Goyang, with both groups performing live for the first time; it streamed worldwide through M2 and Mnet Plus. The groups share a fictional universe the company calls ACE (A2O Cosmic Epic), set on a resource-rich planet named Soulite. Lee Soo-man also presented 4D Gaussian Splatting — multi-camera footage combined into a 3D space so new camera moves can be added after filming, developed with Samsung Electronics — and an AI-powered fan platform called Blooming Talk. Producer Yoo Young-jin, known for work with aespa and NCT, oversees both groups' music and performances.

Summarized from The Korea Times and Korea JoongAng Daily by A2O Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — OCT 8

A2O Entertainment opens official channels for its first domestic debut project

Lee Soo-man's A2O Entertainment, where he serves as Key Producer and Visionary Leader, opened the groups' official channels on Oct. 8 and announced the simultaneous debut of boy group A2O SOUL and girl group A2O LITE. Pre-debut promotion included a teaser on Mnet's M Countdown, a mood film for A2O SOUL, and individual member trailers for A2O LITE.

Summarized from Sports Kyunghyang by A2O Wire staff.
